How a project runs

About two weeks for a typical site.

You see and approve each stage before I start the next one.

  1. Day 1

    Discovery

    We talk about your customers, your goals and what the site must do.

  2. Days 2–3

    Design

    Phone and desktop designs of the key pages, for your approval.

  3. Days 4–7

    Build

    The working site, with payments, bookings or forms connected.

  4. Days 8–9

    Test & content

    Checks on real phones, speed and security. Your content goes in.

  5. Day 10

    Launch

    The site goes live, and I show your team how to update it.

Counted in working days. A small site can launch in about a week. A large platform with many integrations takes longer, and I'll tell you before we start.
