About me

One accountable partner, from first sketch to launch day.

I'm Fanuel Furaha, a software engineer, web developer and web designer based in Nairobi, Kenya.

I help businesses and organisations of every size get online with websites that look professional, load fast and bring in results.

For the past three years I have designed, built and maintained websites for organisations across Kenya, from a health intelligence platform for policymakers to a travel agency that sells safaris and international trips online.

My certifications cover the whole job: user experience design from Google, front-end and back-end development from Meta, and cloud development from AWS. I design the look, build the systems behind it, and host it securely, so you never have to coordinate between a designer, a developer and an IT company.

How I think about your website

Your website is your best employee. It never sleeps, greets every visitor warmly, answers their questions instantly, and turns their interest into a booking, a sale or a call, at 3 a.m. as well as 3 p.m.

  • Built around your goal

    Every page has one job: move the visitor one step closer to buying, booking, joining or getting in touch.

  • Designed for phones

    Most Kenyans browse on their phones, so I design for the small screen first.

  • Fast and secure

    Pages load quickly on mobile data, and customer details and payments are protected.

  • Easy for your team

    Your staff update content, products and prices themselves, without calling a developer.

How I work

Seven clear stages. You approve each one before I move on.

Like building a house: plans first, then construction, then inspection, then moving day.

  1. Discovery

    I learn about your customers, brand, competitors and goals.

  2. Wireframes

    Simple sketches of every page and key journey for your approval.

  3. Design

    The full look: colours, fonts, photography and layout, refined with you.

  4. Build

    I develop the site and connect payments, bookings and your systems.

  5. Testing

    Checks on phones and browsers, plus speed, security and test payments.

  6. Launch

    Go live with no downtime, analytics in place and Google notified.

  7. Support

    Staff training, then updates, backups and improvements.
